Account & Data Deletion — Muslim Leveling

App: Muslim Leveling (id.muslimleveling.muslim_leveling)

1. Cloud Account & Data

To request deletion of your Muslim Leveling account and all associated cloud data (profile, progress backup, achievements), send an email:

📧 muslim.leveling@gmail.com
Subject: Muslim Leveling Account Deletion Request

Please include the Google email address you used to sign in to the app. We will delete your account and all associated Supabase cloud data within 7 days.

2. Local Data

All on-device data (quests, streaks, settings) can be deleted instantly:

  • Android Settings → Apps → Muslim Leveling → Storage → Clear Data, or
  • Simply uninstall the app.

3. What Gets Deleted

  • Supabase cloud backup (XP, level, streaks, quest logs, cosmetics)
  • Learning progress and achievements tied to your account
  • Google sign-in association

Anonymous crash reports (Sentry) contain no personal identifiers and expire automatically.
